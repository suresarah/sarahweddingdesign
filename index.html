About me

Hi, I'm Sarah

I've been a user experience designer for the past 10 years, and I'm still a senior product designer at a cloud software company. I got married recently, loved designing and decorating my own wedding, and wanted to do it for others.

I'm very sentimental, and I love illustrating and bringing ideas to life. I've lived in south Denver for 8 years, so if your event is in Colorado, I can help you set it up too.

Over the pandemic, my husband, his dad and I built a she shed and greenhouse in our backyard out of old windows. So no idea is too big.

FAQs

Can I just get one design done?

Yes, of course! The price depends on the level of detail and what you want. Simple pieces start around $250.

Do you set up the decor on the day?

Yes! If your event is in Colorado, I can help set everything up so it's ready before your guests arrive. We'll talk through what you need on our first call and I'll include it in the quote.

Can you design something that isn't wedding related?

That's totally okay! I love design in general and would be happy to help you out.

Why don't you have many reviews yet?

2025 is the year I officially started in the wedding industry. I've been a user experience designer for 10 years and love designing events. If you book me, maybe you could write me a review ;)

Are you real?

Yes! I live in south Denver and have been here for 8 years. If your event is in Colorado, I can even help you set it up. If you're in Denver and want to meet for coffee for our first chat, we can do that too.

Can I keep the things you make?

If it's custom, of course! If you want the digital files, I can provide those too. When we talk about what you want, I'll include everything in the quote.

What if I don't want everything in the quote?

Let me know and I'll adjust the quote to fit.
